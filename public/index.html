Rotating savings circles. Everyone pays in each round, and one member collects the whole pot. Keep going until everyone has had a turn.

Live contributions count only after Roqqu confirms the exact payment. Contributors can attach optional proof. Payouts are tracked here; this app does not send crypto payouts.
