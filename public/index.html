CirclePay

Rotating savings circles. Everyone pays in each round, and one member collects the whole pot. Keep going until everyone has had a turn.

Practice mode: no Roqqu key is set, so payment links open a simulated page and no real money moves.

Contributions are collected with Roqqu payment links and only count once Roqqu confirms them. "Mark paid" is for cash or bank transfers received outside Roqqu.
